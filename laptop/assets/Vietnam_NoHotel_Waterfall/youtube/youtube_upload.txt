No hotel, no plan, and then I lost my camera. 📷 This is the night three village boys in Vietnam searched for me in the dark and brought it back, asking for nothing. 🙏

I rode a scooter out of Buôn Ma Thuột to find Đray Nur Waterfall, with no hotel and the sun already going down. 🛵🌊 I swam under the falls, got thrown out at 6 pm, and a stranger I met in the water invited me to camp with him by the river. ⛺🔥 In Europe, someone would have sold my camera. Here, the kids brought it to our campfire. Then came the party at the fire, a night in a hammock under the stars, a riverside gym with one hammer, Phở Bò for breakfast... and a small debt to the village kids. 🍜

📍 Đray Nur Waterfall, Đắk Lắk, Vietnam - 21 km (about 45 min by scooter) from Buôn Ma Thuột 🛵
🎟️ Entry: 50,000 VND
