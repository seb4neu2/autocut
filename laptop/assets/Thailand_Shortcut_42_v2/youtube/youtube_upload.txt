A boat to the lake in Khao Sok National Park costs 2,000 baht. I said no and took a "shortcut" through the Thai jungle instead. 🌴😅 Half a liter of water, no path, no plan... and two stray dogs who decided to be my guides. 🐕🐕

It took 42 minutes of climbing, sliding and swearing to reach the water. Was it worth it? Watch until the end, because the day did not stop at the lake: the locals let me sleep in a fishermen's hut for free, shared their water with me and took me into the pitch-black lake at night with a torch and a diving mask. 🔦🌊

📍 Khao Sok National Park, Thailand - about 3 hours (173 km) by scooter from Phuket 🛵
💸 Budget for the day: 0 baht
